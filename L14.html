How the topic test works

Criterion A: Knowing and understanding. It covers the whole unit.

Strand i
Recall, state, outline and describe what you know.
Strand ii
Apply what you know to problems, including situations you haven't seen before.
Strand iii
Interpret data and information to make judgements, backed by science.

Each section starts easy and gets harder. Attempt every question: the hard ones are where the top levels are.

Quick-fire quiz · part 1

Write your answers, then check

  1. List the seven levels of classification. Kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, species.
  2. Name the three domains of life. Archaea, Bacteria, Eukarya.
  3. Name the three types of adaptation. Structural, behavioural, physiological.
  4. What is variation? The differences between individuals of the same species.
  5. Why is a bat a mammal, not a bird? It has fur and feeds its young milk.

Quick-fire quiz · part 2

Write your answers, then check

  1. List the five steps of natural selection. Variation, selection, reproduction, inheritance, time.
  2. Why did dark peppered moths become common? On sooty trees, birds ate more light moths, so dark ones survived to breed.
  3. How many chromosomes are in a human gamete? 23.
  4. What is a zygote? A fertilised egg, with DNA from both parents.
  5. What is a mutation? A mistake made when DNA is copied.

Be the marker

"Describe how the number of spots can be passed from a male guppy to his offspring."

Answer A
The babies get spots from their dad because they are related to him.
Answer B
The father passes on genes for spots in his sperm. The sperm fertilises an egg, so the baby gets genes from both parents.
Answer C
Spot number is coded for by genes on chromosomes. The male makes sperm, gametes with one copy of each chromosome, so one allele for spots. At fertilisation a sperm joins an egg to make a zygote with two copies of each chromosome, one from each parent. The zygote divides into the baby, which inherits the father's spot genes.

Rank them from weakest to strongest. Then: what would B need to add to become C?

How they'd be marked

From "states" to "describes"

  • A just states a link. No science words: low level.
  • B outlines the idea: genes, sperm, fertilisation. Correct, but brief: middle level.
  • C describes the whole process in order: genes on chromosomes, gametes with one copy, fertilisation, zygote with two copies, inheritance. Top level.

To move up a level: use the key words, put the steps in order, and link them back to the trait in the question.

Practise with data

Beetles on a volcanic island

A volcano erupts and covers the island's pale sand in dark ash. Birds eat the beetles they can see. (Example data.)

Practise with data

Questions

  1. Which colour was most common before the eruption? Light, 85%.
  2. Describe how the population changed. Use the data. Dark beetles rose from 15% to 90%; light beetles fell from 85% to 10%.
  3. Explain the change using natural selection. Beetles varied in colour. On dark ash, birds saw and ate more light beetles (selection). More dark beetles survived to reproduce, and their offspring inherited dark colour. Over 20 generations (time), dark beetles became common.
  4. A student says, "The beetles turned dark because they needed to hide." Do you agree? No: the dark beetles were already there. The ash didn't change any beetle; it changed which ones survived.
